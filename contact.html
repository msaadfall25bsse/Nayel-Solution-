Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Nayel Solutions.

What is the typical turnaround timeline for custom software?
Turnaround depends on project scope. A standard web portal or mobile app MVP is delivered in 4 to 8 weeks. Comprehensive enterprise ERP or Hospital HMS implementations range from 8 to 16 weeks, broken down into bi-weekly milestone sprints.
Do you sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA) to protect our IP?
Yes, absolutely. We sign strict bilateral NDAs before reviewing any proprietary business documentation, source code, or product designs. All developed intellectual property is 100% owned by the client upon completion.
How are project payments structured?
We use transparent milestone-based payments (e.g. 25% Advance Scoping, 25% UI/UX & Architecture, 25% Sprint Development, 25% Final QA & Deployment). We accept wire transfers, credit cards, Stripe, and direct bank remittances across USA, Pakistan, and GCC.
Do you provide post-launch maintenance and support?
Yes, every deployment includes 30 to 90 days of complimentary bug warranty and SLA support. We also provide monthly maintenance tiers covering server DevOps, security patches, database backups, and feature enhancements.
Can Nayel Solutions integrate with our existing legacy systems?
Yes. Our engineers specialize in RESTful/GraphQL API middleware and database connectors for Oracle, SAP, Microsoft SQL Server, MySQL, and custom legacy on-premise systems.
